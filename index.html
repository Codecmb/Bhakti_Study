Dedication & Acknowledgment

With Gratitude

This Academy was created by Madhuha Dasa A. (HDG) with gratitude to his spiritual master, Hridayananda Dasa Goswami Maharaja, whose instruction, encouragement, and example inspired and motivated the development of this project.

The Academy is offered as an educational tool to help students systematically study Śrīla Prabhupāda's books and become increasingly proficient in the science of Kṛṣṇa consciousness.

Purpose of the Academy

Study · Remember · Realize

The purpose of Academia Master Siddhānta Gauḍīya is to provide a systematic environment for studying, remembering, understanding, and applying the teachings presented by Śrīla Prabhupāda, with the goal of developing a mature understanding of Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava siddhānta and becoming proficient in the science of Kṛṣṇa consciousness.

Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta · Madhya-līlā 8.128

kibā vipra, kibā nyāsī, śūdra kene naya
yei kṛṣṇa-tattva-vettā, sei 'guru' haya

Translation: “Whether one is a brāhmaṇa, a sannyāsī or a śūdra—regardless of what he is—he can become a spiritual master if he knows the science of Kṛṣṇa.”
